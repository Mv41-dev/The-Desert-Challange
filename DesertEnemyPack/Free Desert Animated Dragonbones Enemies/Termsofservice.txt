Hi folks, here is a small free pack of desert themed enemies animated in Dragonbones!

The pack features 10 animated Battlers;

Ankh, Canopic_Cat, Dino, Dung_Dropper, Jackal, Mummy_Head, Mystic_Cat, Tumble_Imp, Vulture and Worm.

It comes with a 'Preview' folder in each battler's section with gifs named after each motion the battler has.
